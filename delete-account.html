Delete Your Account and Data

Ruzn: Read, Write & Publish by RH Studio PK

Last updated: November 15, 2026

You can ask RH Studio PK to delete your Ruzn: Read, Write & Publish account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to riderhunter301@gmail.com from the same email address you use to sign in with Google in the app.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my Ruzn account".
  3. In the message, write your account name (as shown in the app). If you only want some data removed (for example your reviews or one of your published books) say which.
  4. We will confirm your request by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

What may be kept

  • Records of copyright complaints you filed or that concern your books, and security or abuse-prevention records, may be kept for up to 12 months (or longer if the law requires) so we can handle disputes and prevent fraud.
  • Backup copies are removed on our normal backup cycle, within 30 days after deletion.
  • Books or text already downloaded for offline reading on other users' devices are not affected.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset your Advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.
Tip: You can also remove data without deleting your account: delete your own books or profile picture inside the app, or email us asking to remove specific data. Uninstalling the app removes data stored only on your device.
