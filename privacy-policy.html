1. About the App

Ruzn: Read, Write & Publish is a digital reading and publishing platform. Readers can discover, read, download, review and bookmark books and follow authors. Authors can upload and publish books (PDF or EPUB), manage their profile and view reading analytics for their own books.

Developer / data controller: RH Studio PK

Email: riderhunter301@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide or that comes from your Google account

The App uses Sign in with Google (through Firebase Authentication). When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account;
  • A unique user ID assigned by Firebase.

We do not receive or store your Google password.

2.2 Content you create

  • Profile details you choose to add, such as a bio and a profile picture;
  • Reviews, ratings and follow relationships;
  • Books you publish as an author: title, description, categories, language, age rating, cover image and the manuscript file (PDF or EPUB);
  • Copyright reports you submit about a book, including your name, email, your relationship to the work, an ownership statement, details and an optional evidence link.

Your name, profile picture, reviews and published books may be visible to other users of the App.

2.3 Activity and usage information

  • Your library, bookmarks, reading progress and notifications;
  • Reading and download counts for books, used to show statistics and to give authors analytics about their books (aggregated, not a list of individual readers);
  • Reader settings such as theme and preferences, and text highlights, which are stored on your device.

2.3a Device and technical information

  • A device identifier hash: we create a one-way, salted SHA-256 hash of your Android ID. It is used only to limit how many accounts can be used on one device so we can prevent fake accounts, fake reviews and abuse. We do not store the raw Android ID.
  • Advertising ID and related ad-related device information, collected by the Google AdMob SDK (see Section 4);
  • Technical data such as IP address, device model, operating system version and app-integrity signals, processed by Google Play Services, Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) and our servers to keep the service secure.

2.4 Information we do not collect

The App does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, SMS or call logs, camera or microphone data, or files on your device other than the files you explicitly choose to upload (for example a cover image or manuscript).

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in;
  • To provide the core features: browsing, reading, downloading, library, bookmarks, reviews, following authors and publishing;
  • To show you notifications (for example new books, new followers or reviews, or the status of your submission);
  • To review submitted books and handle copyright complaints and takedown requests;
  • To provide authors with analytics about their own books;
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, fake accounts and violations of our Terms & Conditions;
  • To show ads and measure their performance through Google AdMob;
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App and to comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the App), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improvement), consent (where required, e.g. for personalised ads and notifications) and legal obligation.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display banner ads and rewarded ads. AdMob is operated by Google LLC.

  • AdMob and its partners may collect and use the Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and improve ads, to limit how often an ad is shown, and to prevent fraud.
  • Ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we request your consent before showing personalised ads using Google's consent tools.
  • We do not give advertisers your name, email address or the content of your reading activity.

How to control ads:

  • On your Android device go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (path may vary) to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation;
  • Manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With other users – your public profile (name, photo, bio), your reviews and, if you are an author, your published books are visible to other users;
  • With service providers listed above, who process data on our behalf;
  • With copyright claimants / authors – when handling a copyright complaint we may share the complaint details with the affected author, and the author's identity may be disclosed to the complainant as needed to resolve the matter;
  • For legal reasons – to comply with law, legal process or enforceable government requests, or to protect the rights, safety and property of RH Studio PK, our users or the public;
  • In a business transfer – such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the App, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Backup copies and logs are removed within a reasonable period.

How to request account and data deletion: email riderhunter301@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Ruzn account". We will verify the request and delete your account and associated personal data (profile, library, bookmarks, reading progress, reviews, notifications, avatar and, if you are an author, your uploaded books and cover images) within 30 days. You can also remove your own published books and profile picture in the App.

We may retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security records and records of copyright complaints.

You can also uninstall the App at any time. Data stored only on your device (such as highlights and reader settings) is removed when you uninstall or clear app data.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated access, Firestore security rules, Firebase App Check and hashed device identifiers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your Google account secure.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Users must meet the age requirement of a Google account. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at riderhunter301@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Books carry an age rating chosen by the author. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are configured for a general audience.

We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined by the CCPA, other than through the standard use of Google AdMob described above, which you can opt out of using the controls in Section 4.
